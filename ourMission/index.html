The Dream

We are eight seniors from Punahou School in Hawai‘i working together in Ke Kilohana, our capstone computer science course. Each of us brings different experiences and coding skills to the table. This project is both a graduation requirement and a chance to apply what we’ve learned throughout our highschool careers to real life. By combining technology with sustainability, we hope to design an augmented reality kalo garden bed that connects innovation with tradition and creates lasting resources for future students.
