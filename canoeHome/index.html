    Welcome!

    Welcome to our Kalo Garden Project—a living space where tradition meets innovation. Rooted in the values of mālama ʻāina, this project seeks to create a raised bed for kalo while integrating modern tools like multi-layered topographic mapping, moisture sensors, and interactive statewide garden data. More than a buildout, our goal is to cultivate a hands-on learning environment for students of all ages, offering easy-to-follow guides, collaborative opportunities, and open-source resources for schools across Hawai‘i.
